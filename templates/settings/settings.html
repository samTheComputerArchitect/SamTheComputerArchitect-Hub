Vim mode in the paper editor
Normal/insert/visual modes with the full vim keymap. Press j then k quickly while typing to leave insert mode; :w saves and compiles, :q goes back to the project. "+y yanks to the system clipboard, "+p pastes from it.
Keep the standard hjkl motions
The remap always sends j left, k down, l up and ; right. Turn this off to unbind h, j, k and l from their usual meanings and use jkl; as the only way to move.

The paper editor picks these up the next time the page loads. The remap only reorders motions in normal and visual mode: while typing, letters insert as usual, and the jk chord is what leaves insert mode.
